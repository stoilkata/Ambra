Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy describes how the Ambra Android app (“Ambra”, “we”) handles personal data. Ambra is a small project. This page exists so the app can be listed and so Google sign-in can be offered; it is not legal advice.

Who we are

Ambra is operated by the developer of the app. Contact: skalugerov@gmail.com.

What the app does

Ambra lets you keep motorcycles and cars, log maintenance and upgrades, track equipment, and attach invoice files. It is not a social network. Other people only see a vehicle if you turn on “Visible to other owners”.

Data we store

  • Account. Email address, and a display name if you set one.
  • Google sign-in. If you choose Sign in with Google, Google shares your email, name, and profile picture. We request only the openid, email, and profile scopes. We do not read Gmail, Drive, or other Google services.
  • Garage data. Vehicles, service entries, equipment, settings, and invoice files you upload.
  • Public vehicles. If you mark a vehicle public, signed-in users can read that vehicle and its service history. Your email stays private. Invoices, notes, and paperwork dates stay private.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a Supabase project (hosted Postgres and file storage). Access is limited by row-level security so one account cannot read another account’s private rows.

How we use it

Only to run Ambra for you: sign you in, show your vehicles, and (if you opt in) show a public vehicle to other signed-in users. We do not sell data, run ads, or use Google user data for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Data stays until you delete it. You can delete individual vehicles, entries, equipment, and invoices in the app. Settings → Delete account removes the user and all associated Ambra data, including invoice files.

Your choices

  • You can use email and password instead of Google.
  • You can keep every vehicle private.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app.
